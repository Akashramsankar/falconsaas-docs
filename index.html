Sprint Capacity Planning & Forecast for Jira Cloud

How much can this team really take on, is the running sprint in trouble, and whose work will not land — answered from the data already in your Jira board.

Realistic capacity

Per person, from sprint dates, working days, hours, time off, holidays, focus factor and allocation — not one team-wide number.

Live risk

A 0–100 health score with its factors shown, required pace, scope change since baseline, and ranked spillover candidates.

Per-person forecast

Each member's own past throughput sampled against the hours they have left. Names the items that will spill, and whose they are.

Runs inside Atlassian

No servers of its own, no external data egress, no analytics, no AI. Your data stays in your Jira site's region.
